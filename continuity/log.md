## 2026-08-15 — Chapter 1: IN PENCIL

**DATE, SEASON, CLOCK.** Second week of April, a **Thursday**, their second year. Cherry blossom on the far bank of the Asagi only, four days later than usual. School 13:40 to 15:00; shop threshold 16:22; closing ritual begins 19:41; bell 19:55; consultation 19:56; cut and service 20:00 to 20:44; cape off 20:45; he leaves 20:47. Takafumi due back at nine. **Next appointment written: Thursday, three weeks on, 19:30.**

**THE CUT GIVEN, AND HIS NAPE.** Men's cut (cut, shampoo, finish), **¥3,800**, forty minutes. No razor and no shave: the razor is the licensed barber's monopoly and it is not on this ticket - it is ch5.
- **In:** square-cut nape, chain salon by Kita-guchi, booked by his mother. Fringe standing off the whorl.
- **Craft order run in full:** consultation in dry hair (behind the chair, both looking at the mirror) - paper neck strip - cape, second snap position, arms under - spray damp - wide-tooth comb for the natural fall, then the **cutting comb out of the blue jar** for the work - sectioning back/sides/top - guide section - cut once, never twice - the corner off around the ear with the shear tips - thinning shears, 30-tooth, two passes, never near the ends - over-direction, point-cutting, slide-cutting - cut **around** the whorl, never through - clippers 0.8 mm up the nape with the wrist scoop - sideburn points taken to needles with the clipper corner - **shampoo AFTER the cut**, forward-lean basin, water tested on her own wrist, one hand between water and forehead, two passes - hot towel snapped once, chin upward, pressed at temples and bridge, four seconds - towel-dry, blow-dry with the comb leading in the direction of growth - talc and neck duster - **hand-mirror check, two seconds** - cape off, one hand at the neck and one gathering inward - brush down the back.
- **Out, and this is the series' clock: his nape is now SHIZEN - soft, tapered, POINTED, with NEEDLE SIDEBURNS and a fringe broken into fine points.** It changes at **p21 P6** and every panel after that in the series can be dated from the back of his head. His whorl is **counter-clockwise, set left of centre**, and she cut around it.

**THE POSTER.** Weathering band 1-4: **flat, near-new, one small air bubble under the surface at the jaw.** Seeded in **exactly two panels** - p08 P2 (cropped by the door frame, half out of view) and p27 P2 (cropped by both the window frame and the panel edge, further occluded by Tōma's own shoulder). Coarse dot screen both times, **no legible text**, never clean, never whole. **Nagi does not look at it once, on any of the twenty-seven pages** - including the panel where she is writing his name with his face lit three times life size behind him. Zero spoken references to its condition (0 of the 5 allowed across ch1-31).

**MOTIFS SEEDED, AND THEIR STATE.**
- **The appointment book (title motif).** 2B pencil from Bungudō Ishizuka, ¥90. Names LEFT column, times RIGHT. Book content is now specified exactly for the artist: the only legible writing anywhere is **NARUSE / 19:30**; every other mark is an indistinct pencil trace, and the one cancellation is an erased grey ghost. Three book panels on p27 - see DEVIATIONS.
- **The hot towel.** One beat, p23. Snapped once, four seconds, temples and bridge. **The second temple press is NOT spent - that is ch5.**
- **Hair on the floor / the sweeping.** Seeded p01 (SHA-SHA, the broom, the bent-lipped dustpan). The floor is the chapter's clock: clean at 19:41, drifting by 20:29, **still drifted in the final panel with the broom leaning where she left it.** The chapter deliberately does **not** end on the broom, which leaves ch2 free.
- **The second chair.** In frame and empty at **p01 P1, p03 P5 (full tall panel), p09 P2, p12 P6, p14 P6, p18 (splash)**. Nothing is set down on it. **Zero dialogue about it** - and none is legal before ch23.
- **"HOW SHORT?"** Seeded p11 P6, said once, its own bubble, unpadded, never ironic, at the end of the consultation. Not said outside the shop.
- **The pole.** One pole panel (p27 P6) and one SFX (p03 P6, stuttered: UIIN... UI-IN...). Crack drawn in the casing. Tōma asks about the sound on p16 with the pole not in frame. Nobody offers to fix it.
- **The till that sticks.** Her private opening trick (lift the front edge a centimetre, then push) at p03 P1; **GAKON and one hip-bump** at p26 P4, with somebody already at the counter.
- **The paper neck strip.** p12 P3, drawn large, unremarked, never skipped.
- **The two steel pins.** Deliberately NOT spent - set down on the counter at p07 P1 as part of the state-switch. The teeth beat belongs to ch2.
- **The empty frame on the licence wall.** Not shown. It is ch3's seed and it stays ch3's.

**MONEY.** Ticket **¥3,800**, quoted out loud before he sits. Paid with a five-thousand note, **¥1,200 change**, counted back into his hand, **rung into the till normally**. There is **no brown envelope yet** - that starts ch3-5. Float counted twice: **¥31,600**. Blade tin count written on the masking tape: **61**, less nine tomorrow (nine heads a day; the blades are her father's razor work, not hers). Korokke from Nikuya Hirai **¥90**, cold since six.

**ADDRESS LADDER, CH1 STATE - and the asymmetry this chapter installs.**
- **She calls him nothing.** "You", "chin", "head down", "ear". She addresses the head, not the person. She does not say his name once in twenty-seven pages, including after he gives it.
- **He calls her "KOMORI-SAN"**, once, at the door (p26 P6) - the ch1 rung, paid exactly where the ladder wants it, mid-chore, with an object in her hands. He has been able to read it off the gold lettering on the glass (**BARBER KOMORI**, reversed from inside, the MORI re-lettered in a slightly different gold) since he walked in. **He has had her name all evening; she has to ask for his.** Nobody remarks on it.
- **Zero given names between the leads.** Nagi calls Yaeko "Grandma". Yaeko does not use Nagi's name on the page.

**RATIONS SPENT / UNSPENT.**
- **DOKI: ZERO.** Substituted throughout by held beat panels, per the showrunner ruling.
- **KAAA: zero. Non-working touches between the leads: ZERO** - all four pre-ch16 touches remain unspent. Every contact in this chapter is her hands doing a job.
- **Beat panels: three.** p09 P2 (the decision), **p20 P4 - the tsumuji straight down from above, the most intimate panel in the chapter, and it carries nothing at all**, p25 P3 (both of them in the same mirror; automatic trigger). None adjacent, none on page 1.
- **Direct-gaze panels: exactly ONE** - p27 P2. Everything else routes through the glass or through the back of his head.
- **Chibi: 5** (p03, p05, p07, p16, p23), spread, never grouped, never announced. **No page is built on a gag.**
- **True asides - Tōma 3 of 3** (p12 P6, p15 P2, p21 P6): all questions or conditionals, none an imperative, none acknowledged. **Nagi 3 of 3** (p20 P6, p22 P5, p25 P5): all imperatives or measurements, no subject, none a question, none acknowledged. Everything else of hers is a working mutter (uncapped). **"grow faster" not spent.**
- **The one acknowledged aside: not spent.** Nobody in this chapter hears anybody.
- **Body distance held at the ch1-5 rung throughout:** a full arm's length, elbow locked, body squared to the chair back, feet planted apart. **She never touches the chair** - that is the ch6-11 rung.

**REGISTERS, AS EXECUTED - the mechanical rule later writers inherit.**
- **THE CAPE IS THE SWITCH.** Cape on = the chair register. Cape off = public.
- **Tōma in public (doorway p08-p09, and p26 P1 to the last page): complete sentences, NO contractions, and NOT ONE QUESTION.** Verified line by line.
- **Tōma in the chair: his first question is p15 P1, "WHAT DOES THE BACK LOOK LIKE?"** - his chin is on his chest, he can see nothing, and it is the first question he has asked anybody in two years. **She does not answer it.** He restates it flatly at p17 P2, which triggers THE PAUSE; her hands answer instead; it is paid at p25 with the hand mirror. Nothing earlier steals it. After it the questions run at full rate.
- **His first three contractions in the series land at p23 P3, p24 P1 and p25 P4** - mortified, then unguarded, then honest - and the public register re-latches the instant the cape comes off. From here on, "no contractions" from Tōma is the reader's alarm.
- **Nagi at school: two spoken lines, both deflections, both ending on an exit.** No self-pity anywhere. Nobody says her name in that building.

**ENSEMBLE TOUCHED.**
- **Komori Yaeko** - on-page, upstairs, all chapter. Speaks through the ceiling twelve times and **never comes down the fourteen stairs**. Drawn once, p01 P3, leaning out of the top doorway, not using the handrail. She knows nothing about the boy. Her last line - that the door went twice and gave both notes - is the only crack in that, and it is not followed up. Tea at 20:30, not before and not after; tonight it was sixteen minutes late and nobody said why.
- **Komori Takafumi** - **absent, zero panels.** At the barbers' association meeting on Thursday evenings, out by half six, back about nine. Referenced three times. He does not know Tōma exists. His till-hitting is shown only as a chibi thought-image inside Nagi's head (p03 P2).
- **Nunokawa Koharu** - p04 P3, P4 and p05 P6. Speaks three times, always to the row, never to Nagi by name; Nagi does not learn her name. Both bag straps on both shoulders in every panel.
- **Yagisawa Gaku** - heard only as a voice on the PA at p04 P5, badly paced on purpose. Not seen, not named to anybody in the room. Proper introduction is still ch4.

**SIGNAGE - every piece of legible text in this chapter, and there are only four.** The vending machine card (**SOLD OUT**); the blade tin's masking tape (**61**, older numbers scribbled through and unreadable); the appointment book's single new line (**NARUSE / 19:30**); the door glass (**BARBER KOMORI**, reversed). Everything else - the poster, the classroom board, the framed licences, the blade supplier's calendar, the magazine covers, the banknotes, the pencil barrel, the korokke paper - is explicitly cropped, turned, face-down or illegible in the panel description. No panel anywhere in this chapter invites the artist to invent a word.

**DEVIATIONS LOGGED (three).**
1. **Appointment book, three panels on p27 against a cap of two** (p27 P1 opened, P4 the hand and the erase-and-rewrite, P6 the final image). Allowed once, because arc.md pins ch1's final page to the book and the erase-and-rewrite beat is locked. **The two-panel cap resumes at ch2.**
2. **The service runs p10 to p25 - sixteen pages - against style.md's 6-9 page full cut.** arc.md mandates ten pages of cutting for ch1 specifically, because this is the chapter that teaches the reader the ritual they will feel the absence of from ch3 onward. **Not repeatable: from ch2 the 6-9 page law applies.**
3. **The consultation's hands go into dry hair before the paper neck strip** (p10 P4 precedes p12 P3), so the strip is not literally the chapter's first physical contact, as style.md motif 10 describes it. The craft order in series.md requires this and wins. The strip remains the first contact **of the service**, is drawn, and is never remarked on.

**DELIBERATE SEEDS FOR LATER WRITERS.**
- **p27 P4: she erases his name and writes it again, straighter.** She corrects the writing. She does not correct hair. Nobody says so. Available to pay any time after the reader learns that a good barber does not cut hair twice.
- **p26 P6: he has read BARBER KOMORI off the glass and used it; she has to ask for his name on the next page.** Available forever, never to be pointed at.
- **p22: nobody in this family is allowed to touch the basin tap.** Sets up the ch42 argument about basin temperature.
- **p20 P6: "nobody stands behind it this long."** Left unexplained.
- **p17 P6: "everybody gets a mirror. at the end."** The chapter's own promise, paid eight pages later at p25.
- **p26 P5: the kettle filled at 19:52 and switched on at 20:46.** The only measurement of what tonight cost her, and nobody remarks on it.
- **p18 splash: "the salon never asked me anything."** The chapter's thesis, said once, never repeated.

**TITLE.** IN PENCIL reads on p01 as the shop's ordinary habit - the book, the blade count on the tape, the float. On p27 it is the fact that the only record of him anywhere is erasable, and she is the one holding the eraser.
