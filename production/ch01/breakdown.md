LOGLINE: Five minutes before closing, a girl nobody at school can name puts her hands into the hair of the boy whose face is lit on the pole outside her window, and never once looks at the pole.

CONTINUITY IN:
- Series opening. continuity/log.md is empty; nothing is inherited.
- Second week of April, a Thursday, second year. Nagi and Tōma are the same year at Kōyō, different classes, and have never spoken.
- Takafumi is at the barbers' association meeting until nine. Yaeko is upstairs with the baseball on.
- Chair two has not been sat in since Yaeko stopped cutting. Nobody remarks on it, in this chapter or any chapter before ch23.
